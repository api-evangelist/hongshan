Internal Promotion or External Hire? How Startups Should Decide Published September 14, 2026 Between 27 and 46 percent of executive transitions are regarded as failures or disappointments two years in, according to a 2018 McKinsey study . A large company absorbs those odds, but a startup making its first two or three senior hires is betting a meaningful share of its leadership team on each one of them.
