Career Development: A Startup’s Practical Competitive Edge Published September 1, 2026 Canva describes its careers as a rock-climbing wall, a structure of lateral and vertical moves that lets people reach higher without waiting for a rung to open above them. Its internal mobility and reskilling program, Pathways, facilitated more than 200 internal moves in 2024 , while the company scaled through hypergrowth. The lesson for founders is direct: the companies that keep their best people are the ones that visibly grow them.
