Why User Experience Is Critical for Startups Published September 13, 2026 When Melanie Perkins founded Canva in 2013, professional design software took months to learn. Canva compressed a new user’s first success to minutes: open the product, choose a template, drag, publish. That obsession with the first session now serves a community of 200 million-plus monthly active users.
