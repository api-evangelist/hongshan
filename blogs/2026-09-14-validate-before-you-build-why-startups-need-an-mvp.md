Validate Before You Build: Why Startups Need an MVP Published September 14, 2026 Before Canva became a design platform used across the world, it was a yearbook design tool. Melanie Perkins and Cliff Obrecht believed design software was too hard for ordinary people, so they tested that belief with Fusion Books, a small product that let Australian schools build their own yearbooks. Real schools paid, real students used it, and the founders learned precisely why.
