A Founder’s Guide to Data-Driven Decision-Making Published September 1, 2026 Meituan runs one of the largest real-time decision systems on earth. Its dispatch engine assigns more than 60 million orders a day across more than five million active couriers, and optimizing each assignment against live conditions cut average delivery time by roughly 21 percent. That decision gets made tens of millions of times a day, and every one of them runs on evidence.
